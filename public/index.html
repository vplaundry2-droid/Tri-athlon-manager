How the model works

CTL = previous CTL + (completed estimated TSS − previous CTL) / 42.
ATL uses the same recurrence with 7.
Form for a morning = previous evening CTL − ATL.

Estimated TSS = completed hours × intensity factor² × 100. Session-RPE load = completed minutes × simulated RPE, reported separately. RPE is perceived effort; it is not the same as pain.

Durability, tissue response, adaptation, bone stress and recovery coefficients are experimental gameplay assumptions. CTL / ATL are load summaries, not injury diagnostics. Progress is gradual; outcomes are reproducible for a career seed.
